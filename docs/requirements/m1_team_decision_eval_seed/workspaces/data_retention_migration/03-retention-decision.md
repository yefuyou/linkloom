# Data Retention — Final Policy Decision

## Decision

The approved current policy retains active records for 180 days and archived
records for 365 days. The policy distinguishes the two classes rather than
using one uniform period.

## Rejected Alternative

The uniform 90-day deletion proposal was rejected because it would remove audit
history too early and would not preserve the archive class.

## Legal Boundary

Deletion exceptions still require legal sign-off. The policy decision does not
authorize a deletion job to run before that sign-off.

## Approval

The compliance review group approved this policy on 2026-01-18.

# Data Retention — Policy Review

## Review Finding

Uniform 90-day deletion would remove audit history too early and would not
distinguish active working records from archived records.

## Recommendation

The review recommended 180 days for active records and 365 days for archived
records. Deletion exceptions require a separate legal sign-off.

## Migration Constraint

Mapping and dry-run work may proceed, but no record deletion is authorized
until the legal sign-off and exception list are complete.

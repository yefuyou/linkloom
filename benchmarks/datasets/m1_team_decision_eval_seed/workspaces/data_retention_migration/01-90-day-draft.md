# Data Retention — 90-Day Draft

## Proposal

The first migration draft proposed deleting every record after 90 days. It was
intended to reduce storage and was written before the audit-history review.

## Assumptions

The draft assumed that the same retention period could apply to active records
and archived records. It did not contain legal deletion sign-off.

## Supersession

This proposal was superseded by the retention review and the approved policy.
It is not the current retention rule.

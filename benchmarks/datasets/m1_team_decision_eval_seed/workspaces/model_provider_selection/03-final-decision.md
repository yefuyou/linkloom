# Provider Selection — Final Decision

## Decision

The Atlas Lantern team selected Aster A as the approved model provider for the
synthetic pilot and its next integration slice. The selection is an approved
decision, while regional readiness actions remain tracked separately.

## Rejected Alternatives

Borealis B was rejected because the residency exception and additional
operational burden were not acceptable for this slice. Cedar C was rejected
because the review did not establish an auditable region commitment and its
streaming behavior did not meet the adapter requirement.

## Rationale

The decision prioritizes an auditable data boundary and an owned operational
path over the faster early prototype. The office-hours prototype observation
does not supersede this record.

## Approval

The decision was approved on 2026-01-22 by the Atlas Lantern technical review
group. Remaining readiness work must not be described as completed adoption.
